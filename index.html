Common Questions

Frequently Asked Roofing Questions

Straightforward answers to help you make an informed decision for your home.

If your roof is under 15 years old and damage is isolated to a single pipe boot, flashing joint, or a few wind-blown shingles, a targeted repair is usually the most cost-effective solution. If your shingles have widespread granule loss, curling edges, soft decking, or pervasive hail bruising across multiple slopes, a full replacement provides better long-term protection.
Most single-family residential roofs (up to 3,500 sq. ft.) are completely torn off, inspected, felted, and shingled in 1 to 2 days. Steep-pitch roofs, custom standing seam metal installations, or homes requiring extensive wood decking replacement may take 2 to 3 days.
Most standard US homeowners policies cover sudden, accidental damage caused by hail, windstorms, and fallen trees (minus your policy deductible). Summit Ridge Roofing provides a complimentary photo inspection packet and can meet your insurance adjuster at your property to ensure all storm damage is properly documented.
In addition to the manufacturer's Limited Lifetime Material Warranty on shingles, our Summit Ridge Roofing 10-Year Workmanship Warranty covers installation labor, flashing details, valleys, and ridge vents. If any installation-related leak occurs within 10 years, we repair it at zero cost to you.
You do not need to be home for an exterior roof inspection—we can inspect the roof, email you high-resolution photos, and review the estimate by phone or in person. During installation day, we only ask that vehicles are moved out of the driveway and pets are kept safely indoors.
